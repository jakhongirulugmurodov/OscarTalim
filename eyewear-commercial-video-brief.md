# Eyewear Commercial — 10s Cinematic Video (9:16 Vertical)

Production brief and ready-to-use AI video generation prompts for a 10-second
photorealistic eyewear commercial. Optimized for **Google Veo 3**, **OpenAI Sora**,
**Kling 2.x**, and **Runway Gen-4**.

---

## Concept

**"The world, in focus."** A young man's blurry city world snaps into vivid clarity
the moment he puts on sleek black-frame glasses. Premium fashion-ad aesthetic,
golden-hour finish, subtle confidence.

- **Duration:** 10 seconds
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 (vertical — Reels / TikTok / Shorts)
- **Style:** Photorealistic, cinematic, shallow depth of field
- **Character:** One consistent young man (early 20s, modern casual-smart outfit)

---

## Shot Breakdown

### Shot 1 — Blurry World (0–3s)
- **Camera:** First-person POV, static with slight handheld sway
- **Scene:** Bright modern city street at daytime; entire frame soft and out of focus
- **Action:** His hand lifts sleek black-frame glasses up toward the camera/eyes
- **Audio:** Muffled, low-pass-filtered city ambience (distant traffic, blurred voices)

### Shot 2 — The Snap Into Focus (3–5s)
- **Camera:** Still POV; glasses slide on in slow motion
- **Action:** The instant the frames settle, the world racks into crystal-clear focus;
  colors turn vivid and saturated; a gentle sun flare skims across the lens edge
- **Audio:** A subtle, satisfying "click" on the focus snap; ambience opens up to
  crisp, full-frequency clarity

### Shot 3 — Hero Walk (5–10s)
- **Camera:** Cut to third person. Smooth slow dolly-in from medium shot to
  close-up on his face and glasses
- **Scene:** Same street, golden-hour light, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh,
  premium fashion-ad look
- **Action:** He walks toward camera in slow motion, confident; ends with a subtle smile
- **Audio:** Minimal upbeat electronic track rises and carries to the end

## Consistency & Quality Checklist

- [ ] Same actor face, hair, outfit, and glasses across shots 2 and 3
- [ ] Glasses: sleek, matte-black rectangular frames — identical in every shot
- [ ] 9:16 framing safe zones: keep face within center 80% for platform UI overlays
- [ ] No on-screen text, watermarks, or brand logos (add branding in post)
- [ ] Focus snap lands exactly on the "click" sound
- [ ] Export: 1080×1920 (or 4K vertical), 24–30 fps, H.264/H.265
